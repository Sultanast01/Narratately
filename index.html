Install as an app

iPhone: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen."

It'll open full-screen like a normal app, no browser bars.

About

Narrately is a free resource — reads PDFs, Word docs, and text files aloud right in your browser. Free to use. Your books are saved to your account so they follow you across devices.

Built in public. View it on GitHub ↗ — a star there helps a lot if you find it useful.
